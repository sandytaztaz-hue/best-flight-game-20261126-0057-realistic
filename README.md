REAL FALCON — Ace Combat Run: a photorealistic jet-combat campaign that plays offline in a desktop browser from its own static files. You are Falcon-1, newest pilot of the Sky Falcon squadron aboard the carrier Kestrel, flying six sorties across a whole sea war against the raider fleet of the Crimson Wake — sunset intercepts, island dogfights, a fleet shield action, a night strike, a storm canyon run, and a final duel with the hidden enemy ace GHOST above a foggy dawn sea. Lock-on + AIM-9 missiles, flares, afterburner, exploding jets and sinking ships, a hangar shop with seven upgrades, three save slots, and a full cockpit or chase view over real photographic skies and seas. Every scene element on screen is photographic imagery — real skies, real seas, real jets — never flat cartoon shapes.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests, no install — static files only.

## How to play

- **WASD / arrow keys** — steer · **SPACE** — cannon · **F or click** — fire missile (needs a target) · **C** — drop flares · **SHIFT** — afterburner (drinks fuel) · **V** — cockpit / chase camera · **P** — pause · **M** — mute · **ENTER** — confirm
- Hold the target inside the centre bracket until the lock tone turns solid green, then fire. Enemies pop flares to break your lock; yours do the same for you.
- Earn credits per kill and per mission grade (S/A/B/C), spend them in the hangar on seven upgrades: missile rack, flare pack, armor plating, afterburner engine, long-range radar, drop tank, cannon upgrade.
- Fuel and hull turn every sortie into a decision — run dry or get shot down and the sortie fails. Three save slots keep your campaign (localStorage).
